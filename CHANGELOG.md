# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Infrastructure in the event calendar: roads, tracks, bridges, tunnels, stations and depots that become available or can no longer be built, including new versions of town roads and depots, railroad crossings, traffic lights, alleys and barriers; the catenary, tram catenary, bus lane and traffic light switches. Variants that change in the same year are one row, with all names in the tooltip.
- New type filters: roads, tracks, bridges and tunnels, stations, depots. The filters are now in two rows (type; engine and specialization).
- "No longer buildable" status for infrastructure.
- Info windows for infrastructure (every variant with its picture, years, the game's description, cost, upkeep and top speed as in the construction menu) and for inflation steps (what gets more expensive, the factor now and by year).
- The vehicle info window follows the height of its card and opens in the right half of the screen, the list in the left half, with a gap in the middle.
- The list is narrower (the filters are in two rows), the engine column too.
- Search by name (also among the variants of a group).
- Clock tooltip: at a calendar speed other than 1x, a line that the reporting period is not a calendar year.
- What is available when the game starts: under its own earlier year, or, without a year of its own, in the group "Available from the start" at the top.
- The look of the game: the list and its filters in a panel like the game's lists, cards on the game's card background, only the game's colors (follows color themes).

## 1.0.1

- Clock tooltip: shorter, with the date when the next reporting period starts instead of the cycle length in calendar days (at calendar speeds other than 1x, e.g. ≈ 3 months at 0.25x, it looked like a wrong year length).
- "Reporting year" is now "reporting period" in the UI and the description, in all languages: the cycle is a calendar year only at calendar speed 1x.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Reporting-period clock in the bottom bar (next to Earnings).
- Event calendar: all vehicles and multiple units that appeared in or left the store since the game started and will do so later, by year, with game time until each future year; opens at the current year.
- Columns: vehicle type, picture, model, status, engine, cargo specialization; filters by type, engine and specialization.
- Milestones: construction cost inflation, catenary.
- Vehicle details window as in the vehicle store, next to the list.
- UI and mod description in all 13 game languages.
